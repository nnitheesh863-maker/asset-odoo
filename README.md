# AssetFlow – Enterprise Asset & Resource Management System

![AssetFlow Banner](https://via.placeholder.com/1200x400)

## 📌 Project Overview

**AssetFlow** is an Enterprise Asset & Resource Management System designed to help organizations digitally manage and track their valuable assets and resources.

Instead of using manual records or spreadsheets, AssetFlow provides a centralized platform where companies can manage asset information, assign assets to employees, monitor asset status, schedule maintenance, and generate reports.

The system improves asset visibility, reduces asset loss, and helps organizations use their resources efficiently.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Many organizations face difficulties in managing assets manually:

- Assets are difficult to track
- Employees may forget to return assets
- Maintenance schedules are missed
- Asset history is not properly maintained
- Reports take more time to prepare

AssetFlow solves these problems by providing a complete digital asset management solution.

### Employee Management

- Manage employee details

### Asset Assignment

- Assign assets to employees

- Manage returns

- Schedule maintenance
- Track repair history
- Monitor maintenance costs

### Reports

- Employee reports

- Department reports
